# 📚 Arcane Archives

Arcane Archives is a modern digital library built to showcase my original books and writing projects in a clean, immersive, and visually engaging way.

The website serves as a central place where readers can discover my stories, explore book collections, and stay updated on upcoming releases.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📖 Dedicated pages for original books
- 🎨 Premium dark-themed UI
- 📱 Fully responsive design
- ✨ Smooth animations and hover effects
- 📚 Beautiful book showcase layout
- ⚡ Fast and lightweight

---

## 📚 Featured Books

- **Arjun's Odyssey** – A fantasy adventure series filled with magic, relics, and ancient mysteries.
- **Files They Buried** – A crime thriller series following investigator Kabir Verma as he uncovers hidden truths behind unsolved cases.

More books and series will be added in future updates.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of Arcane Archives is to provide readers with a dedicated platform to explore my stories while allowing me to present my work through a modern and interactive web experience.
